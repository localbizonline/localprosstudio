## Usage Notes

- **Style consistency:** All prompts use dark backgrounds with amber/gold accents to match the page design
- **Target generators:** Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Ideogram, or FAL AI
- **Aspect ratios:** Most images are 16:9 (`--ar 16:9` for Midjourney), phone submission is 4:3
- **South African context:** Add "South African" to prompts where people are shown for local relevance
- **Screenshot/Graphic prompts:** Added realistic UI mockups with annotation arrows, callouts, labels, and comparison formats for more authentic-looking marketing graphics
