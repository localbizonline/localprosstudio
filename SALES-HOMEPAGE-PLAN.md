## Tone & Voice Guidelines

### DO:
- Write like you're talking to a friend over coffee (or beers)
- Use short sentences. Short paragraphs. Line breaks.
- Be specific with numbers ("18 months" not "a while")
- Acknowledge their pain genuinely
- Swear mildly if it feels natural ("hell of a lot easier")
- Use South African context (Rands, local business types)

### DON'T:
- Sound corporate ("industry-leading", "synergy", "leverage")
- Overcomplicate with jargon
- Make promises you can't keep
- Fake urgency with countdown timers
- Use generic stock photos

### Voice Examples:

**Instead of:**
> "Our comprehensive digital marketing solutions enable businesses to achieve optimal online visibility."

**We say:**
> "We make sure people can actually find you online. That's it."

**Instead of:**
> "Leverage our proprietary systems for enhanced customer acquisition."

**We say:**
> "We figured out what works. Now we do it for you."

## Technical Implementation Notes

### Route
- Create as `/sales` or `/home-sales` for A/B testing against current homepage
- Component: `SalesHomePage.tsx` or `HomeSalesLetterPage.tsx`

### Styling
- Dark mode throughout (`bg-neutral-950`, `bg-neutral-800`)
- Amber accent color for callouts and CTAs
- Large typography with good line-height
- Generous section spacing (`py-16 md:py-24`)
- Mobile-first responsive design

### CTAs
- All CTAs link to WhatsApp with pre-filled message
- Example: `https://wa.me/27832336716?text=Hi%2C%20I'm%20interested%20in%20getting%20started%20with%20Local%20Pros%20Studio`

### Performance
- Lazy load images below the fold
- WebP format with fallbacks
- Compress all images <200KB
